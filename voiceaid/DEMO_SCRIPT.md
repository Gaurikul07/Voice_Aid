# VoiceAid Demo Script (3-5 minutes)

**Before the demo:** build and install the app (see README), install an offline
English and Hindi text-to-speech voice (Android Settings > Text-to-speech),
charge the phone, raise the volume. Do the demo on a real phone if possible.
Keep a backup plan: if audio fails, show the on-screen text (that is a feature).

**0:00 - The problem (30 s)**
"In rural areas an injury can happen far from a clinic, with weak internet.
People may not read well, and may use harmful remedies. VoiceAid gives simple
spoken first-aid steps in Hindi and English, with no internet."

**0:30 - Why offline matters (20 s)**
"All instructions are bundled inside the app. Let me turn on airplane mode now."
(Turn on airplane mode, close and reopen the app.)

**0:50 - Choose an emergency (30 s)**
"Big picture buttons, minimal text." Tap **Burns**. Point out the red warning banner
("Call 112 for..."), step counter, icon, text.

**1:20 - Audio guidance (60 s)**
"The voice reads this step." Press **Play**, then **Replay**. If recordings are
installed, say "this is a recorded voice"; otherwise say "this is the phone's
offline voice, and the app tells the user so". Explain Pause works for
recordings; for phone voice use Stop/Replay. Press **Next**: "Nothing advances
by itself. The user decides." Show the red-bordered **DO NOT DO THIS** step
(label + icon, not colour only).

**2:20 - Language (30 s)**
Go back, tap the language button (हिन्दी). "The whole interface and the
instructions switch to Hindi, and the choice is remembered." Open Burns again
and play a step in Hindi. If the Hindi voice is not installed, show the message
and the text fallback.

**2:50 - Choking and safety (30 s)**
Tap **Choking**: "Adult, child and infant have different steps, so the app asks
first. Infants get back blows and chest thrusts, never belly thrusts."

**3:20 - Emergency numbers (30 s)**
Tap the red 112 button. "112 is India's national emergency number from the
Ministry of Home Affairs. The app never calls by itself: it asks for confirmation,
then opens the dialer." Show the custom contact option and the label that older
numbers are unverified.

**3:50 - Limitations and future (40 s)**
"This is a prototype. The medical content is adapted from public sources and has
not been reviewed by clinicians. We have no voice recordings yet, TTS depends on the
phone, and we have not done user testing. Next: medical review, local voice
recordings, illustrations, more languages and field tests."

**Likely questions:** "Is it medically validated?" - No; flagged for review.
"What if there is no Hindi voice?" - App detects it where possible and shows text.
"Does it collect data?" - No health data; only language/voice settings and an
optional contact stored on the phone.
